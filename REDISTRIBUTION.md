# Publication basis and redistribution scope — 10 November 2026

Public sharing of student academic assignments and projects for resumes and
professional portfolios is permitted under the IIT Jodhpur institute policy
reported by the project owner. This copy is prepared for that stated purpose.

## Policy basis and evidence

The project owner supplied the following description on 10 November 2026:

> The publication basis is the IIT Jodhpur institute policy permitting students
> to publicly share academic assignments and projects for resumes and professional
> portfolios.

This is the owner's description, not a quotation from an institute document.
No official policy text, URL, version, or clause was supplied in the repository
or conversation, and a targeted official-site search did not locate a matching
policy. The reported permission is recorded as supplied, not independently
verified or expanded into a blanket redistribution grant.

Its stated scope is public sharing of student academic assignments and projects
for resumes and professional portfolios. It does not establish an open-source
license, permission to relicense inherited material, commercial reuse rights,
or rights for others to modify or redistribute the work. Whether the policy
covers third-party framework code, supplied tests, and teaching documents is not
established by the description. No project-wide LICENSE has been added.
